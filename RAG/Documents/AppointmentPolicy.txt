HealthcareAI Appointment Policy

Patients can book appointments with available doctors.

Appointments should be cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment.

If an appointment is cancelled less than 24 hours before the scheduled time, the patient should contact the hospital support team.

Patients should arrive 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

Emergency cases should not use the normal appointment process. Patients should contact emergency medical services or visit an appropriate emergency department.
